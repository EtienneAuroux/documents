# Study On - Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 2, 2026
**Last updated:** April 2, 2026

Study On is an AI-powered tutoring application developed by Etienne Auroux. This policy explains what data Study On collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

---

## 1. Data we collect

### Account information

When you create an account via email, we collect your email address. Student accounts created through a guardian invite code do not require an email address.

### Conversations

Conversations are **ephemeral**: message text, images, and audio are processed in real time and are **not stored on our servers**. A brief text description of media content may be generated to improve learning insight extraction.

### Learning insights

Study On stores learning insights and exercise progress to personalise your experience over time.

### Usage data

Each conversation or exercise records a timestamp. No message content is stored. This data is used to calculate session duration, enforce guardian-set screen-time limits, and provide statistics to the guardian.

### Payment information

Payments are processed entirely by Stripe. Study On does not receive or store your credit card details. We store only your Stripe customer ID, subscription status, and credit balance.

### Safety alerts

If the AI detects a potential welfare concern (such as emotional distress), a safety alert is created for the linked guardian. The alert contains only the signal type and timestamp, no conversation content is included.

---

## 2. How we use your data

- To provide the tutoring experience
- To personalise learning based on stored insights and progress
- To process payments and manage subscriptions and credits
- To enable guardian oversight: usage statistics, topic breakdowns, screen-time limits, and safety alerts
- To enforce content guardrails for student safety

---

## 3. Third-party services

Study On relies on third-party services for authentication, AI processing, payment handling, and hosting. No analytics or tracking services are used.

---

## 4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with third-party services only as described above and only to the extent necessary to provide the service.

Guardian accounts can view their linked children's usage statistics (session counts, topic breakdowns, credit usage) and safety alert types. Guardians **cannot** view conversation content.

---

## 5. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

---

## 6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.
